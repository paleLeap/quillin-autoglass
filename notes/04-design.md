# Design Notes

Direction: minimal, sleek, high signal. An auto glass customer is usually stressed
and in a hurry. The site should answer "can you fix this, how fast, how much" in
the first screen.

## Principles
- One clear action per screen.
- Phone number always reachable without hunting.
- Type does the work; minimal decoration.
- No carousels, no stock-photo hero with a headline over it unless the photo is theirs.
